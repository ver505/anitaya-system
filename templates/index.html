SECTION 4 — RESPONSIBLE AI & LIMITATIONS STATEMENT

AniTaya is engineered exclusively as a predictive decision-support system to guide agricultural resource planning and harvest estimation. It does not constitute a loan warranty or yield guarantee. Climatic parameters are calibrated using Southeast Asian tropical proxies (Indonesia) from the FAO dataset. In compliance with data privacy ethics, AniTaya gathers zero personally identifiable information (PII), farmer identification records, or land titles.
